PORTABLE/STATIC LASER ARRAYS

Mobile Boom for Laser Array Systems Cat # A5

The components from the home page are used on this boom assembly which is adaptable to a standard trailer or can be used with the traffic style trailer for easy deployment and legally towed to site.

The array is kept in a Penguin case for safe transport and quickly hitches to the boom after the trailer is deployed in position over the preferred belt system.

Deployment on static belts can be carried out by site maintenance, with the system monitored and calibrated remotely from any office or vehicle office.
