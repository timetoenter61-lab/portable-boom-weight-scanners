Portable boom system

BUILT TO DEPLOY WITHOUT COMPROMISE.

This trailer is the mounting and levelling system for the laser array.

The array is kept in a Pelican-style protective case for safe transport and quickly hitches to the boom after the trailer is deployed. This prevents collisions that could damage the array.

Batteries and electronics are housed in the toolbox, while the tablet operates wirelessly. On this unit, the enclosure interfaces with the crusher for power and speed. The new system incorporates these functions in a 7-inch HMI attached to the array.
